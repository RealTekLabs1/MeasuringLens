Privacy Policy for MeasuringLens

Last Updated: August 20, 2026  |  Effective Date: August 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RealTek Labs LLC, a [STATE] limited liability company (“RealTek Labs,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information in connection with the MeasuringLens mobile application for iOS (the “App”).

By downloading or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Summary

MeasuringLens is designed to work without collecting your personal information. In plain terms:

  • We do not operate any servers that receive your data. The App has no backend of our own.
  • Camera images are never saved or transmitted. They are processed in your device’s memory and immediately discarded.
  • Your custom recipes stay on your device. We cannot see them.
  • The App connects to the internet only for Apple’s purchase and subscription services, and when you tap a link.
  • We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and we do not sell or share personal information.

The sections below describe each of these points in detail, including the limited information Apple handles on our behalf.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

RealTek Labs does not collect, receive, or maintain any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, postal address, or telephone number.
  • Your precise or approximate geographic location.
  • Photographs, video frames, or any other camera imagery (see Section 3).
  • Contacts, calendars, health data, financial account details, or files from your device.
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA), cross-app or cross-site browsing history, or any other data used to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, or social-media software development kits (SDKs). Because the App does not track you, it does not present the App Tracking Transparency prompt.

3. Camera Access and Image Processing

The App requires access to your device’s camera in order to display a live camera feed with measurement overlay lines. When you grant camera permission, iOS presents you with our stated purpose; you may revoke this permission at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Camera, though the App’s core functionality will not operate without it.

Camera imagery is processed entirely on your device and in memory only. Frames from the camera feed are not written to persistent storage, are not saved to your Photo Library, are not transmitted off your device, and are not accessible to RealTek Labs or to any third party. The App does not request or receive write access to your Photo Library and does not save any images to it. Frames are discarded as soon as they are rendered.

4. Information Stored on Your Device

The App allows you to create and save custom recipes and related preferences. This information is:

  • Stored locally on your device only.
  • Never transmitted to RealTek Labs, and never accessible to us.
  • Deleted permanently when you delete the App from your device.

If you have enabled iCloud Backup or encrypted local backups, your device may include App data in those backups. Backups are created and controlled by Apple and by you, not by RealTek Labs, and are governed by Apple’s privacy policy and iCloud terms. We recommend exporting or noting any recipes you would not want to lose, since we have no ability to recover data from your device.

5. Subscriptions and Purchases

The App offers an auto-renewing subscription. All purchases and subscription management are handled by Apple through the App Store using Apple’s StoreKit framework.

  • We never receive your payment information. Credit card numbers, billing addresses, and Apple Account credentials are collected and processed solely by Apple. We have no access to them.
  • The App communicates with Apple’s servers in order to present available subscription options, complete a purchase you initiate, and verify your current subscription status so that paid features can be unlocked.
  • We receive limited transaction information from Apple in connection with your subscription, which may include an anonymous transaction identifier, the product purchased, and the subscription status and renewal date. This information is used only to determine entitlement to paid features and to maintain our business records. It does not identify you by name.
  • Apple also provides us aggregated sales and subscription reports that summarize purchases across all users. These reports do not identify individual users.

Apple’s handling of your purchase information is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy, available at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

6. Diagnostics and Analytics Provided by Apple

We do not embed any analytics software in the App. However, if you have chosen to share analytics and diagnostics with app developers in iOS (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may make available to us aggregated, non-identifying information about App performance — for example, crash logs, energy usage, and counts of app launches or impressions.

This information is collected by Apple at the operating-system level under your control, is provided to us in aggregate form, and is not used by us to identify you. You can turn this sharing off at any time in iOS Settings.

7. Information Collected by Apple and the App Store

Your download of the App, your App Store activity, and your use of Apple services are subject to Apple’s own terms and privacy practices. RealTek Labs does not control and is not responsible for Apple’s data practices. Please review Apple’s Privacy Policy for details.

8. Links to Other Websites

The App contains links to this Privacy Policy, our Terms of Service, and potentially other resources. Selecting a link will open the referenced page in your browser or an in-app browser view. Any third-party site you visit is governed by that site’s own privacy policy, and we are not responsible for the content or practices of sites we do not operate.

9. Data Security

Because RealTek Labs does not collect or store personal information on servers we control, there is no user data in our possession to be exposed in a breach of our systems. Information you store within the App resides on your device and is protected by your device’s own security features, including your passcode, Face ID or Touch ID, and iOS file encryption. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of information stored on your device.
