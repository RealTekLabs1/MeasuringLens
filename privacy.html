Privacy Policy for MeasuringLens

Last Updated: September 10, 2026  |  Effective Date: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RealTek Labs LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company (“RealTek Labs,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information in connection with the MeasuringLens mobile application for iOS (the “App”).

By downloading or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. Summary

MeasuringLens is designed to minimize the collection of personal information. In plain terms:

  • We operate no servers that receive your data. The App has no backend of our own, no user accounts, and no database of users.
  • Camera images are never saved or transmitted. They are processed in your device’s memory and discarded.
  • Your custom recipes stay on your device. We cannot see them.
  • Motion and orientation sensor readings stay on your device. They are used only to position the on-screen overlay.
  • The App connects to the internet only for Apple’s purchase and subscription services, and when you tap a link.
  • We use no third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, or tracking software, and we do not sell or share personal information.

The sections below describe each of these points in detail, including the limited subscription information that Apple handles and provides to us.

3. How Information Flows: Your Device, Apple, and Us

Because the App has no backend, it helps to distinguish three separate layers:

  • Processed on your device only. Camera frames and any still image captured for measurement, the identification of the vessel and its edges, motion and orientation sensor readings, your custom recipes, and your App preferences. None of this is transmitted to us, and none of it is accessible to us.
  • Handled by Apple. Your Apple Account, payment information, App Store activity, subscription billing and renewals, refunds, and any device backups you have enabled. Apple’s handling of this information is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy, available at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.
  • Received by RealTek Labs. Aggregated, non-identifying sales, subscription, and app-performance reports that Apple makes available to developers, and anything you choose to send us directly, such as an email to our support address.

4. Camera Access and Image Processing

The App requires access to your device’s camera in order to display a live camera view with measurement overlay lines and to capture a still image used to position those lines against your vessel. When you grant camera permission, iOS presents you with our stated purpose; you may revoke this permission at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Camera, though the App’s core functionality will not operate without it.

Camera imagery is processed entirely on your device and in memory only. The live camera feed and any still image captured for measurement are used to identify the vessel and derive the information needed to place the measurement overlay. They are not written to persistent storage, are not saved to your Photo Library, are not transmitted off your device, and are not accessible to RealTek Labs or to any third party. The App does not request or receive write access to your Photo Library and does not save any images to it. Images are discarded from memory once the measurement overlay has been rendered or you leave the camera view.

No biometric data. The App does not perform facial recognition and does not collect, capture, or store any biometric identifier or biometric information — including any scan of face or hand geometry, fingerprint, voiceprint, or retina or iris scan — as those terms are used in the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act, the Texas Capture or Use of Biometric Identifier Act, and comparable laws.

5. Device Motion and Orientation Sensors

The App uses your device’s gyroscope, and the device orientation information iOS derives from it, to position and stabilize the measurement overlay relative to the vessel. These sensor readings are processed on your device in real time, are not written to persistent storage, and are not transmitted to RealTek Labs or to any third party.

6. Information Stored on Your Device

The App allows you to create and save custom recipes and related preferences. This information is:

  • Stored locally on your device only.
  • Never transmitted to RealTek Labs, and never accessible to us.
  • Deleted permanently when you delete the App from your device.

If you have enabled iCloud Backup or encrypted local backups, your device may include App data in those backups. Backups are created and controlled by Apple and by you, not by RealTek Labs, and are governed by Apple’s privacy policy and iCloud terms. We recommend exporting or noting any recipes you would not want to lose, since we have no ability to recover data from your device.

The Standard EULA described in Section 2 requires you to remove the App from an Apple device before you sell or transfer that device to someone else. Removing the App also removes the recipes and preferences stored on it, which prevents that information from passing to the new owner.

7. Subscriptions and Purchases

The App requires an auto-renewing subscription. All purchases and subscription management are handled by Apple through the App Store using Apple’s StoreKit framework.

  • We never receive your payment information. Credit card numbers, billing addresses, and Apple Account credentials are collected and processed solely by Apple. We have no access to them.
  • The App communicates with Apple’s servers in order to present available subscription options, complete a purchase you initiate, and verify your current subscription status so that access can be unlocked.
  • Transaction information stays on your device. When you subscribe, Apple’s StoreKit services provide the App with the transaction and entitlement information needed to determine whether you have access — which may include a transaction identifier, the product purchased, and subscription status and expiration or renewal dates. The App evaluates this information on your device. Because we operate no servers and do not perform server-side receipt validation, this transaction information is not transmitted to RealTek Labs and we do not store it.
  • Apple provides us aggregated reports. Through App Store Connect, Apple gives us aggregated sales and subscription reports summarizing purchases across all users. These reports do not identify individual users.

Apple’s handling of your purchase information is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy, available at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.
