Privacy Policy for MeasuringLens

Last Updated: July 3, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how RealTek Labs LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with your use of the MeasuringLens mobile application (the “App”).

1. Our Commitment to Your Privacy

RealTek Labs LLC is committed to protecting your privacy. This App is designed to provide measurement overlay functionality without compromising your personal information.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

The core principle of MeasuringLens is zero data collection. Specifically:

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect any personally identifiable information from you, such as your name, email address, phone number, location data, or any unique identifiers linked to you.
  • No Camera Data: The App uses your device’s camera in real-time for measurement estimation and overlay. However, no video feeds, images, or frames from your camera are recorded, stored, transmitted, or processed by us or any third party. All camera-related operations are performed locally on your device and are transient.
  • No Usage Data: We do not collect any data about how you use the App, such as features accessed, duration of use, or any crash reports.
  • No Tracking: The App does not contain any analytics tools, advertising trackers, or third-party SDKs that collect data about your usage or behavior.

3. How Information (None) Is Used

Since we do not collect any personal information or usage data, there is no information for us to use, share, or sell. The App’s functionality operates entirely on your device without external communication.

4. Data Security

Given that MeasuringLens does not collect or store any user data, there is no data to secure on our servers. Your privacy is inherently protected by the App’s design.

5. Children’s Privacy

The MeasuringLens app is not specifically designed for or targeted at children under the age of 13. We understand the importance of protecting children’s privacy online.

  • No Knowing Collection: We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 years of age (or the relevant age in your jurisdiction).
  • Parental Contact: If a parent or guardian believes that MeasuringLens has inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately using the information provided below, and we will take steps to address the concern.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.
