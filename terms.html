2. Description of the App

MeasuringLens uses your device’s camera to display a live camera view of a drinking glass or similar vessel, overlaid with adjustable measurement reference lines. Once you visually align these lines with the vessel, the App presents estimated volume markings so the vessel may be used as an approximate measuring aid. The App also allows you to save custom recipes and related preferences locally on your device.

The App is a visual estimation aid. It is not a calibrated measuring instrument, does not physically measure anything, and its output depends entirely on how you position the reference lines, the shape and dimensions of the vessel, the camera angle, and lighting conditions.
